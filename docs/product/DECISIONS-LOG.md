| 2026-07-26 | Layout UI/UX review (Orchestrator asked whether the six modes are practical at all). Finding: **which layout is right is arithmetic, not taste** — compare the viewport's aspect against the page's √2 and the questions answer themselves. The peek is *free* until it eats the vertical slack: 35% on a 393×852 phone against a 28% default, so Half page (top/bottom) draws the music at exactly Single page's size and adds a quarter of the next page — but only 20% is free on a 375×667 phone, where the same default shrinks the music 15%. Single page wastes **51% of a landscape tablet**, where Two pages costs 2% of page size for twice the music. **Fit height has no describable job**: a duplicate of Single page on a portrait tablet, of Two pages on a landscape one, three tiny pages on a phone in landscape, and a 591-pt page inside a 393-pt viewport on a phone in portrait. Turn gestures are one global set across six layouts with different motion axes — Fit width scrolls vertically but turns horizontally, and Fit height's scroll drag *is* its turn gesture. Spec **0041** drafted `proposed` (polish **Q9**; P0.5 / P0.6 reopen candidates) with nine G3 questions; load-bearing ones are cutting Fit height and making **Auto** a real mode. It absorbs the Two-pages-in-portrait question, which was never in this log — the 0036 finding above is the landscape one. |
